# Genome wide and Transcriptome wide Mapping of G-Quadruplex and i-Motif Forming Sequences in *Caenorhabditis elegans*

This repository contains the computational pipelines, processed results, statistical analyses, and visualizations generated for this research project.

The study investigates the genome wide and transcriptome wide distribution of potential G-quadruplex (G4) and i-motif (iM) forming sequences in *Caenorhabditis elegans* (WBcel235).

## Research Overview

G-quadruplexes and i-motifs are non canonical nucleic acid structures formed from guanine rich and cytosine rich sequences respectively. This project computationally maps potential G4 and i-motif forming sequences across the *C. elegans* genome and transcriptome and examines their distribution across genomic and transcriptomic regions.

The analysis integrates:

- Sequence pattern based motif identification
- Genomic and transcript annotation
- Regional motif density analysis
- GC%, G% and C% composition analysis
- Statistical correlation analysis
- Genome wide and transcriptome wide visualization

## Key Results

### Genome wide analysis

- **1,150** potential G-quadruplex sequences (PGS)
- **1,222** potential i-motif sequences (PIS)
- **47,651** annotated genes
- **609** gene associated PGS
- **668** gene associated PIS
- Motifs identified across **952 motif bearing genes**

### Transcriptome wide analysis

- **34,689** transcripts analysed
- **100%** GTF annotation matching
- **76** PGS hits
- **387** PIS hits
- PGS detected in **61 transcripts**
- PIS detected in **359 transcripts**

At the transcript level PIS showed its highest overall mean density in the **3′ UTR** while PGS showed its highest overall mean density in the **CDS**.

The genomic analysis also revealed strong depletion of detected PGS/PIS within annotated exonic sequence and enrichment patterns that varied across genomic regions.
